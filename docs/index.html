BrightPause

A calm break reminder for Windows. Version 1.0.0

Eye break · 20 seconds

Look far away

Find something about 6 metres away and rest your eyes on it until the timer ends. Blink a few times.

20

A heads-up, never a surprise

Ten seconds before a break (thirty before a movement break), a small notice appears in the corner with Snooze and Skip. It never takes the keyboard focus, so you can finish your sentence.

Rest your eyes, then move

Every 20 minutes a calm 20-second break covers your screens: look far away, blink, roll your shoulders. Every third break is a 5-minute movement break. Each one shows a short message, a tip with its source, or a small challenge.

Stays out of your way

Breaks wait while another app uses your microphone for a call, or while a game, a video or another app is full screen. Five minutes away from the keyboard counts as a break. No account, no network, and your settings stay on your PC.

BrightQueue

Short lessons and quizzes, one topic at a time.

Made the same way

A few rules every Bright app follows.

Light

Small downloads and little memory. BrightPause runs one small process between breaks and installs in about 2 MB.

Calm

No pop-ups that grab your keyboard, no ads, nothing that asks for more of your time than the task needs.

Plain about data

Every app has a privacy page written from what its code actually does, in plain words.
