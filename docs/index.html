BrightPause

Eye break · 20 seconds

Look far away

Find something about 6 metres away and rest your eyes on it until the timer ends. Blink a few times.

20

A heads-up, never a surprise

Ten seconds before a break (thirty before a movement break), a small notice appears in the corner with Snooze and Skip. It never interrupts your typing, so you can finish your sentence.

Rest your eyes, then move

Every 20 minutes a calm 20-second break covers your screens: look far away, blink, roll your shoulders. Every third break is a 5-minute movement break. Each one shows a short message, a tip with its source, or a small challenge.

Stays out of your way

Breaks wait while another app uses your microphone for a call, or while a game, a video or another app is full screen. Five minutes away from the keyboard counts as a break. No account, no network, and your settings stay on your PC.

Privacy

BrightPause privacy policy. Last updated 25 November 2026.

BrightPause collects nothing about you and shares nothing with anyone. This covers the Microsoft Store version and the version built from the public source code.

On your PC only

settings.json holds your choices. history.jsonl has one line per break (time, kind, and whether you took, postponed or skipped it) for the counts in settings. events.log notes when a break starts, waits or ends and why, for example the file name of a full-screen app; it stays under about 0.5 MB. Your background picture is kept too, if you pick one. The Store version keeps these under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\ and uninstalling deletes them; the source build keeps them in %APPDATA%\io.github.jordanalbayrak.brightpause\. You can delete either folder at any time.

Microphone

With "Wait during calls" on, BrightPause asks Windows whether another app is recording from a microphone, so a break does not start mid-call. It never records, opens or listens to the microphone itself, and learns nothing about the other app except that it is recording.

Network

The Store version makes no network connections; the Store delivers its updates. The source build contacts GitHub only to check for updates, when you press "Check for updates" or turn on automatic updates (off by default). A source link on a break screen opens in your browser when you click it. No accounts, ads, analytics, crash reports or telemetry.

Children

BrightPause collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes appear here and in the app's source code, where its history shows every version. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
